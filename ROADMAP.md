# ROADMAP

Listed, not built. v0 is the free floor: the format, five nozzles, the hand-run test.

- **Mirror-check authoring.** An adaptive elicitation dialogue that drafts a first Self and Stance
  from a conversation and hands them to the person to mirror-check — never written into the Carry
  by the tool; lands in `carry/proposed/`, a human moves it.
- **Injectors.** Browser extension and MCP paths that place the rendered nozzle output into the
  vendor's slot automatically. v0 is paste-only by design.
- **Round-trip with vendor shelves.** Reading a vendor's own memory/preferences surface back and
  diffing it against the Carry. The Carry is never the target of that write.
- **Drift alert.** A check that a vendor's slot still holds what was rendered; a changed block is a
  flag, never a merge.
- **Encrypted sync.** The Carry on more than one machine, encrypted at rest and in transit, keys held
  by the person.
- **Professional seats.** A practitioner (coach, clinician, teacher) conducting the authoring session
  for a client, with the client holding the file.
- **Pricing.** The paid layer, if any, decided later. The format stays CC0; v0 stays MIT.
